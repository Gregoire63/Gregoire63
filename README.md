## 🍔 Currently building: DishRank

A food app that rates **dishes**, not restaurants.

> _"A 4.2★ restaurant can serve an outstanding burger and a disastrous tartare.
> Average ratings tell you nothing about what YOU should order."_

DishRank ranks every dish independently, so you know exactly what to order —
and helps your group decide where to go together, in 30 seconds.
